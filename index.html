Are you using Early Years Funding?

London Fields publishes separate monthly fees for 15 and 30 funded hours. Funding is stretched across N's 51-week year.

Important: 15 funded hours are stretched to 11.18 hours/week and 30 funded hours to 22.35 hours/week because N is open 51 weeks rather than the government's 38 funded weeks.
